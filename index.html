About

Paden Beck is an 18-year-old founder based in Gilbert, Arizona, building companies at the intersection of premium software and applied AI. He is the founder of Recorou, a boutique web development and AI systems studio that designs and ships revenue-generating websites and automation systems for Arizona's growth-stage businesses, and Vigis, an AI-powered compliance monitoring platform that keeps small businesses ahead of regulatory change across all 50 states — scanning 10,000+ federal and state regulations and delivering plain-English alerts filtered by industry, location, and size. Paden operates across both bespoke client delivery and scalable SaaS — a rare combination driven by a conviction that great software is built, not recommended. He writes code daily, ships fast, and believes the best products come from founders who stay close to the craft.
